# Development log

The newest work is at the top.

## October 2026
- Created this repo with a selection of showcase scripts.
- **Jumping fixes:** jumps keep their full height when leaving the ground, and brushing a log mid-air no longer counts as a crash.
- **Simpler ski design:** removed the speed and turning stats so a bigger ski is never a downside; every ski turns at the same rate.
- **Redesigned the draw menu** into three columns: materials, grid, and preview with the buttons.
- **Course v2:** no flat sections, steeper in each zone, solid launch ramps, about 800 themed obstacles, and waterfall, glacier and magma drops.
- **Course v1:** built the three-zone procedural mountain (Pinewood Pass, Glacier Gorge, Ember Peak), with zone gates, lighting that changes per zone, and a finish area.

## September 2026
- Loading screen and intro camera.
- First-time tutorial with spotlight and blur highlights, a guide arrow, and step-by-step prompts.
- Jump and grab trick tutorial.
- Redesigned chest-opening spinner with a reel animation, landing effects and a reward fly-in.
- Shared ski-themed banner module (`SkiPrompt`).
- Lobby music playlist with fades.
